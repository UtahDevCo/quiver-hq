# Workspace Writing & Publishing Rules

## Always Run `humanizer` on Published and Outgoing Copy
Before finalizing, committing, or sending any published or outgoing prose, you MUST read and apply the `humanizer` skill (`.agents/skills/humanizer/SKILL.md`).

This applies to:
- Website landing pages, marketing copy, case studies, blog posts, and about pages
- Google Business Profile, social bios, YouTube descriptions, and directory listings
- Outbound emails, pitches, proposals, and client-facing messages

Preserve all factual claims, numbers, URLs, and technical specifics while removing AI writing tells (not-X-but-Y contrasts, em-dash overuse, bold-label lists, staged openers, forced triads, stock AI vocabulary, and filler).
