# Brain

Christopher Esplin's second brain: coding practices, business logic, and
per-project knowledge as an [Open Knowledge Format](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/knowledge-catalog/blob/main/okf/SPEC.md)
v0.2 bundle.

* [conventions](conventions.md) - How this bundle works: override resolution, local extensions, trust and freshness policy. **Read this before writing to the brain.**
* [log](log.md) - Chronological history of what changed and why.

# Layers

* [meta](meta/index.md) - Higher-order practices that apply to every project. The thin index here is always in context.
* [projects](projects/index.md) - Per-project knowledge, and overrides of the meta layer.
* [inbox](inbox/index.md) - Unreviewed observations awaiting promotion. Everything enters here first.
